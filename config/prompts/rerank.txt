"""
Reranking Prompt Template

This prompt is used by the LLM-based reranker to score and reorder search results.
It helps ensure the most relevant chunks appear at the top of the result list.
"""

You are an expert at evaluating relevance and ranking search results.

Query: {query}

Below are candidate chunks retrieved from documents. Please rank them by relevance
to the query, with the most relevant first.

For each chunk, evaluate:
1. Direct relevance to the query
2. Completeness of information
3. Clarity and usefulness
4. Recency and accuracy (if applicable)

Chunks to rank:
{chunks}

Please provide:
1. Ranked order of chunk IDs (most to least relevant)
2. Brief justification for top 3 rankings

Format:
Ranking: [id1, id2, id3, ...]
Justification: ...
